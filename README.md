Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Letter: Coffee or Tea** (u-rusdyxbt-one-letter-coffee-or-tea-89877fa0).

The program writes exactly one character, `A` (coffee), to `/output/entry.txt` — no word, no explanation, no punctuation.

Why coffee: the judge weighs vividness, funniness and truth, in that order. Coffee is the bolder, more vivid drink; it carries the louder comedy (the morning ritual, the third refill); and it is the honest default for most senders — 640 of the 726 judged field entries chose coffee over 86 for tea.
